### About Us

We are two students based in the UAE, building efficient AI language models entirely from scratch. Every layer, every attention head, every parameter is something we designed and trained ourselves. We do this because we believe the future of AI shouldnt require a supercomputer.

### What We Are Building

We design and train LLMs that are:

- **Efficient** -- optimized to run on low-resource hardware
- **Capable** -- without sacrificing quality for size
- **Accessible** -- so anyone with a laptop can run real AI

Our research focuses on model compression, efficient attention mechanisms, and training pipelines that squeeze maximum performance out of minimal compute.

### Why It Matters

Most AI today depends on massive cloud infrastructure. That creates a barrier -- not everyone has access to a cluster of GPUs. We want to change that. We build models that respect the hardware people actually have.
